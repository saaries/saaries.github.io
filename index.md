## About Me

Hi! I am currently a third-year PhD student at [City University of Hong Kong](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/), supervised by [Prof. Cong Wang](https://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/~congwang/). 
I received my M.S. degree from [City University of Hong Kong](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/) with an award classification of distinction.
Before that, I obtained my B.Eng. degree from [South China University of Technology](https://www.scut.edu.cn/new/main.htm) and visited [Tianjin University](https://www.tju.edu.cn/index.htm) and [ITMO University](https://en.itmo.ru/) as an exchange student.
My research lies in privacy and security. Specifically, I am interested in searchable encryption and its related leakage-abuse attacks(SE/LAAs), differential privacy (DP), deep learning (DL), and their intersections.
