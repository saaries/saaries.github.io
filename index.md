## About Me

Hi! I am a Senior Research Engineer at Huawei Hong Kong Research Center, working on the end-to-end post-training of 30B+ language models, from data construction and SFT/RL to distributed rollout and evaluation. My current interests include tool-integrated mathematical reasoning, reinforcement learning, agentic model training, and token-efficient inference.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from [City University of Hong Kong](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/) in 2025, supervised by [Prof. Cong Wang](https://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/~congwang/). My doctoral research focused on privacy leakage and mitigation in encrypted search systems. Before that, I received my M.Sc. with Distinction from City University of Hong Kong and my B.Eng. from [South China University of Technology](https://www.scut.edu.cn/new/main.htm).

## Selected Research & Projects

### Tool-Integrated Mathematical Reasoning

- Trained language models with SFT and multi-stage RL to use Python and Wolfram/Mathematica for mathematical problem solving.
- Developed capability-specific training and evaluation across K–12, undergraduate, and competition mathematics and physics.
- Constructed teacher trajectories, filtered invalid tool calls, and cleaned SFT data to improve genuine tool-use capability.

### Token-Efficient Reasoning

- Applied Long-to-Short RL to transfer reasoning capability from a model with a 32K context budget into an 8K-budget model while preserving answer accuracy.
- Designed RL objectives and length penalties to balance compression, accuracy, and premature termination.

### Agentic Model Training

- Studied agent training through SFT, multi-turn RL, tool-use trajectories, environment feedback, verifiable rewards, and trajectory curation.
